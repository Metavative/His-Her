Your Visit

What to expect.

01

Arrive

Come with a clean face if you can, and bring a list of anything you're currently using on your skin.

02

Assess

A proper look under good light, a medical history review and a frank conversation about outcomes.

03

Treat

Numbing where appropriate, and a pace set by your comfort. Most appointments run 30–75 minutes.

04

Review

Written aftercare before you leave, and a two-week review for injectables at no extra charge.
