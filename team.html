What We Stand For

Four rules
we don't bend.

These aren't posters on a wall. They're the reasons people come back, and the reasons we occasionally lose a sale.

01

Honesty First

If a treatment won't get you where you want to go, we say so — even when it costs us the booking.

02

Conservative Dosing

We start light and review at two weeks. Adding a little is easy. Waiting out too much is not.

03

Time, Not Turnover

Appointments are booked with room to breathe. You will never be rushed through a consultation.

04

Continuous Training

We teach this for a living, which means staying current isn't optional — it's the job.
