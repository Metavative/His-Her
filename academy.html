Why Here

Technique is
taught. Judgement
is transferred.

Anyone can show you where the needle goes. What separates a competent injector from a good one is knowing when not to inject, how to read a face that has been treated badly before, and how to say no to a client kindly.

That only comes from watching someone who does this every day. Our instructors run a live clinic — you'll see real complications, real corrections and real consultations, not a slide deck.

Included in Every Program

Four things we
refuse to skip.

01

Real Hands

You practise on live models under direct supervision — not exclusively on pads and mannequins. Confidence is built by doing it with someone watching.

02

Numbers kept deliberately low so every student gets corrected individually. If you're doing it wrong, someone will notice on the day — not months later.

Who It's For

Beginners welcome.
So are injectors
who want to
get better.

Start with Facials or Microneedling to build hand skills and client confidence, then move into injectables. We'll help you sequence it rather than sell you everything at once.

You already have clients and a room. Body Contouring, Microneedling and PRP are the highest-return additions to an existing esthetics practice, and slot into what you already offer.

Your clinical foundation is done. What you need is aesthetic judgement, product knowledge and the commercial side — which is exactly where our injectables programs put the emphasis.

Plenty of students arrive certified but unsure. We run one-to-one refinement days focused entirely on your technique, your dosing habits and the cases that worry you.
